# Cooking recipes

Source items: `plugins/plugin-cooking/src/main/kotlin/net/azisaba/vanilife/cooking/CookingItems.kt`

Each recipe below uses ingredients from the `CookingItems` ingredient list. Quantities are illustrative and scalable for game or design use. Japanese name is included.

- Curry Rice (カレーライス)
  - Ingredients: `RICE`, `ONION`, `CARROT`*, `POTATO`*, `MEAT`* , `MISO` or curry roux substitute using `MISO` (for umami)
  - Steps: Sauté chopped `ONION` until translucent. Add diced vegetables and meat, brown. Add water and `MISO` (or curry roux) and simmer until vegetables are tender. Serve over `STEAMED_RICE`.

- Bamboo Shoot Rice (筍ご飯)
  - Ingredients: `RICE`, `BAMBOO_SHOOT`, `MISO` or light soy seasoning*, `BUTTER` (optional)
  - Steps: Wash `RICE`. Slice `BAMBOO_SHOOT` and sauté lightly with `BUTTER`. Cook rice together with bamboo shoot and seasoning. Serve warm.

- Eel Rice Bowl (うな丼)
  - Ingredients: `STEAMED_RICE`, `PIKE_CONGER` or `GRILLED_AYU`*, `BUTTER`*, teriyaki glaze made from `MISO` + sugar* or pre-made `TERIYAKI_YELLOWTAIL` sauce
  - Steps: Grill or use prepared `PIKE_CONGER`/`GRILLED_AYU`. Brush glaze and serve over `STEAMED_RICE`.

- Fried Horse Mackerel (アジフライ)
  - Ingredients: `SARDINE` or similar small fish (use `SARDINE`/`FRIED_HORSE_MACKEREL` as prepared), `BUCKWHEAT` or breadcrumbs*, `BUTTER` or oil
  - Steps: Coat fillets in flour/egg/breadcrumbs (buckwheat as substitute) and deep fry until golden. Serve with `LETTUCE` or `TOMATO` salad.

- Grilled Ayu (鮎の塩焼き)
  - Ingredients: `FIREFLY_SQUID` or `PIKE_CONGER`/`SKIPJACK_TUNA`*, salt
  - Steps: Salt fish and grill over charcoal until skin is crisp. Serve whole.

- Grilled Mackerel (鯖の塩焼き)
  - Ingredients: `SARDINE` or `SKIPJACK_TUNA`*, salt, `LEMON`* (or `ORANGE` as substitute)
  - Steps: Salt and grill the fish; serve with citrus.

- Grilled Pacific Saury (さんまの塩焼き)
  - Ingredients: `SKIPJACK_TUNA` or `SARDINE` substitute, salt
  - Steps: Salt fish and grill until cooked through; serve with `DAIKON` radish* or `JAPANESE_RADISH` grated.

- Grilled Squid (イカ焼き)
  - Ingredients: `FIREFLY_SQUID` or `SQUID_SUSHI` (raw squid), soy-based glaze (use `MISO` + sugar*), `LEMON`/`ORANGE` optional
  - Steps: Grill squid and brush glaze; slice and serve.

- Hamburg Steak (ハンバーグ)
  - Ingredients: ground meat* (use `SAUSAGE` minced as substitute), `ONION`, `BUTTER`, `CHEESE` optional
  - Steps: Sauté diced `ONION`, mix with meat substitute, form patties, pan-fry in `BUTTER`, top with `CHEESE` if desired.

- Marinated Eggplant (マリネなす)
  - Ingredients: `EGGPLANT`, `ONION`, vinegar* (use `ORANGE` juice small amount as citrus), `OIL` (butter can be used sparingly)
  - Steps: Grill or pan-fry `EGGPLANT`, slice and marinate with vinaigrette and sliced `ONION`.

- Miso Mackerel (鯖の味噌煮)
  - Ingredients: `MISO`, `SARDINE` or mackerel substitute (`GRILLED_MACKEREL`), `ONION`/`GREEN_ONION`
  - Steps: Simmer fish in a sauce of `MISO`, water, and sugar until glazed.

- Miso Soup (味噌汁)
  - Ingredients: `MISO`, `TOFU`* (optional), `WAKAME`* (seaweed), `GREEN_ONION`
  - Steps: Prepare dashi (water with seaweed), add diced ingredients and dissolve `MISO` before serving.

- Nikujaga (肉じゃが)
  - Ingredients: `POTATO`*, `ONION`, `SAUSAGE` or `MEAT` substitute, `MISO`/soy seasoning*
  - Steps: Simmer meat and vegetables in sweet-salty broth until tender.

- Oden (おでん)
  - Ingredients: fishcakes* (use `FRIED_HORSE_MACKEREL` as a base), `DAIKON`/`JAPANESE_RADISH`, boiled egg*, konnyaku* — simmer in light soy/dashi broth
  - Steps: Simmer ingredients in a dashi broth for a long time to absorb flavor.

- Parfait (パフェ)
  - Ingredients: `STRAWBERRY`/`BANANA`/`BLUEBERRY`, `SOFT_SERVE_ICE_CREAM` or `COTTON_CANDY` garnish, `BUTTER`/`CHEESE` not needed
  - Steps: Layer ice cream, fruit, and toppings in a glass.

- Salad (サラダ)
  - Ingredients: `LETTUCE`, `TOMATO`, `CUCUMBER`, `ONION`, optional `CHEESE` or `SAUSAGE` slices
  - Steps: Toss ingredients with dressing.

- Salmon Roe Sushi (いくら寿司)
  - Ingredients: `STEAMED_RICE`, `SALMON_ROE` — serve roe over vinegared rice
  - Steps: Form small rice balls and top with `SALMON_ROE`.

- Sausage (ソーセージ)
  - Ingredients: `SAUSAGE` (prepared) — serve grilled with `ONION` and `PEPPERS`
  - Steps: Grill or pan-fry and serve sliced.

- Sea Urchin Sushi (うに寿司)
  - Ingredients: `STEAMED_RICE`, `SEA_URCHIN_SUSHI` (prepared uni)
  - Steps: Place uni on small rice balls. Serve chilled.

- Seafood Rice Bowl (海鮮丼)
  - Ingredients: `STEAMED_RICE`, `SALMON_ROE`, `TUNA_SUSHI` slices or `SQUID_SUSHI` slices, `SAKE`* optional
  - Steps: Arrange seafood over rice and garnish with `GREEN_ONION`.

- Shaved Ice (かき氷)
  - Ingredients: `SHAVED_ICE` (prepared item), fruit syrups from `STRAWBERRY`/`MELON` extracts
  - Steps: Top shaved ice with fruit syrups and condensed milk*.

- Soba (そば)
  - Ingredients: `BUCKWHEAT` (soba), `GREEN_ONION`, `DIPPING_SAUCE`* (use `MISO`-based broth)
  - Steps: Cook noodles and serve with dipping sauce and garnishes.

- Soft Serve Ice Cream (ソフトクリーム)
  - Ingredients: `SOFT_SERVE_ICE_CREAM` (prepared), `FRUIT` for topping
  - Steps: Serve in cone with toppings.

- Squid Sushi (いか寿司)
  - Ingredients: `STEAMED_RICE`, `SQUID_SUSHI` (prepared)
  - Steps: Place sliced squid on vinegar-seasoned rice.

- Steamed Rice (ごはん)
  - Ingredients: `RICE`
  - Steps: Rinse and steam `RICE` until tender.

- Takoyaki (たこ焼き)
  - Ingredients: `TAKOYAKI` (prepared) or batter from `BUCKWHEAT` flour substitute, `SQUID_SUSHI` or diced octopus*, `GREEN_ONION`
  - Steps: Cook batter in takoyaki pan with diced seafood and green onion.

- Tamago Sushi (玉子寿司)
  - Ingredients: `STEAMED_RICE`, `TAMAGO_SUSHI` (prepared sweet egg)
  - Steps: Slice sweet omelette and serve on rice.

- Teriyaki Yellowtail (ぶりの照り焼き)
  - Ingredients: `TERIYAKI_YELLOWTAIL` (prepared) or `SKIPJACK_TUNA`/`PIKE_CONGER` with teriyaki glaze
  - Steps: Grill fish and glaze with teriyaki sauce.

- Tonkatsu (とんかつ)
  - Ingredients: `TONKATSU` (prepared) or breaded pork cutlet substitute using `SAUSAGE` flattened, `CABBAGE`* or `LETTUCE`
  - Steps: Deep-fry breaded cutlet and serve sliced with shredded cabbage.

- Tuna Sushi (まぐろ寿司)
  - Ingredients: `STEAMED_RICE`, `TUNA_SUSHI` (prepared)
  - Steps: Place tuna slices on vinegar-seasoned rice.

- Udon (うどん)
  - Ingredients: `UDON` (prepared), `GREEN_ONION`, `MISO`/dashi broth*
  - Steps: Heat broth, add noodles and garnish.

- Yakisoba (焼きそば)
  - Ingredients: `YAKISOBA` (prepared) or noodles, `CABBAGE`/`LETTUCE`, `ONION`, `SAUSAGE` or `SAUCE`*
  - Steps: Stir-fry noodles with vegetables and sauce.

Notes:
- Items marked `*` indicate ingredients not explicitly defined in `CookingItems.kt` but common in these recipes; substitute using listed items where appropriate (e.g., use `SAUSAGE` for small meat portions, `BUCKWHEAT` for batter/flour where applicable).
